# ReadThat brand assets

ReadThat uses one original "reading conversation" mark across Android, iOS,
and the web. The open book doubles as a speech bubble: reading at the top,
community discussion at the tail. The warm coral palette keeps the product
playful and energetic without reusing Reddit's mascot, antenna, or wordmark.

## Canonical artwork

- `readthat-app-icon-master.png` — 1024 × 1024, opaque launcher/PWA artwork.
- `readthat-mark-transparent.png` — 1024 × 1024 transparent foreground mark.
- `readthat-app-icon-source.png` and `readthat-mark-transparent-source.png` —
  untouched 1254 × 1254 ImageGen outputs retained for future exports.

## Palette

| Role | Color |
|---|---|
| ReadThat Coral | `#F04424` |
| Paper | `#FFF8F0` |
| Ink | `#17212B` |

Launcher and UI exports are derived from the canonical artwork. Do not edit an
individual platform size; replace the master and regenerate every target so
the product remains visually aligned.

## Generation prompt

Built-in ImageGen, `logo-brand`: an original, vector-friendly launcher icon for
a community reading and discussion app; a warm-white open book combined with a
rounded speech bubble, a deep-navy center crease, and a full-bleed coral field;
centered in the adaptive-icon safe area; no text, face, mascot, alien, antenna,
border, watermark, or Reddit mark.
